Two paths

Pick a side of the term sheet

Same five-year clock. Opposite pressures. Shared progression across fifty levels.

VC Mode

Write the checks

Start with a $1M–$2.5M fund. Bet across 45 startups, defend ownership with pro-rata, and unlock an Opportunity Fund when a breakout hits 3×.

  • Compete against AI VCs with distinct strategies
  • Follow-ons, syndicates, management fees
  • Climb global net-worth and ROI boards

Founder Mode

Build the company

Choose from 10 templates. Hire the team, raise Seed through Series C, ship the roadmap, and negotiate the exit before the board forces your hand.

  • SaaS metrics, churn, and tech debt
  • Cap table, board seats, acquisition offers
  • IPO, strategic sale, or secondary

How it plays

Sixty months on the clock

Short loops. Hard choices. Achievements and permanent upgrades carry across runs.

VC loop

Pick difficulty, scan the market, place bets, then press on when the round is done.

  1. Choose Easy through Expert (Hard/Expert unlock with level)
  2. Invest across sectors; watch dilution and fees
  3. Follow on when portfolio companies raise
  4. Submit your fund return to the global board

Founder loop

Template in, team hired, customers acquired — then fundraising and exit math.

  1. Pick SaaS, DeepTech, GovTech, or Hardware
  2. Ship features, manage burn and churn
  3. Raise rounds without giving away the company
  4. Exit via IPO, acquisition, or secondary
